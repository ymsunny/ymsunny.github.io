Hi! I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science in the Department of Computer Science, Faculty of Information Science and Computing, at the University of Macau, where I began my PhD in Fall 2023. I am a member of the NLP2CT Lab, advised by Prof. Derek F. Wong. Previously, I completed my master’s studies in the same lab under his supervision and collaborated with Prof. Xuebo Liu.

I am currently a visiting research intern at the Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University, guided by Prof. Shuo Wang in Prof. Yang Liu’s group.

My research centers on trustworthy AI, with a focus on multilingual understanding and the translation of non-compositional expressions. I am currently extending this interest to AI for Science and automated research, investigating the reliability of scientific agents throughout the automated research process.

I am deeply grateful to my mentors and to my collaborators and friends Runzhe Zhan and Chi Seng Cheang for their patient guidance, encouragement and support throughout my research journey.
