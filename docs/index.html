Yanming Sun

University of Macau / NLP2CT Lab

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Macau, advised by Prof. Derek F. Wong in the NLP2CT Lab.

My research focuses on machine translation, multilingual large language models, and trustworthy AI. I study how language models handle idioms, why literal translation errors persist, and how to make translation more reliable under noisy retrieval and limited training data.
